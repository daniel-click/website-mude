# Executive Assurance - Design System

## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a **Corporate Modern** aesthetic tailored for high-stakes insurance brokerage. It balances 35 years of technical expertise with an accessible, client-focused interface. The style is characterized by "industrial elegance"—drawing from the founder's background in aeronautical and corporate risk management.

Visual pillars include:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Utilizing dark, high-contrast hero sections to evoke stability and seriousness.
- **Precision:** Sharp grid alignments and ample whitespace to reflect meticulous attention to detail.
- **Trust-Based Photography:** Large, high-quality environmental portraits and corporate settings that humanize the institutional expertise.
- **Strategic Accents:** Using the deep red brand color sparingly but powerfully to direct attention and signal importance.

## Colors

This design system utilizes a high-contrast palette to distinguish between "Institutional Content" and "Actionable Information."

- **Heritage Red (`#8A2429`):** Reserved for primary actions, critical brand elements, and key emphasis in headings. It represents the strength and longevity of the brokerage.
- **Deep Onyx (`#1A1A1C`) & Slate Gray (`#4B4B4D`):** These form the foundation of the dark hero sections and secondary UI elements, providing a sophisticated, industrial backdrop.
- **Functional Neutrals:** A range of soft grays is used for card backgrounds and section dividers to maintain the "clean whitespace" aesthetic required for high readability (`#F9F9F9`, `#EEEEEE`, `#E5E5E5`).

## Typography

The typography system pairs executive authority with digital-first legibility.

- **Headlines:** **Chivo**. Structured, gothic industrial feel with clean digital rendering. Headlines use tight tracking (-2%) at larger sizes.
- **Body Text:** **Inter**. Geometric construction ensuring complex insurance terms remain readable and accessible.
- **Labels:** Inter with increased letter spacing and bold weights for data-sheet aesthetics.

## Layout & Spacing

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid with generous 32px gutters. Section vertical padding at 120px.
- **Base Rhythm:** 8px base scale (8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 64px, 120px).
